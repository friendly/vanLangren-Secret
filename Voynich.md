# Voynich Manuscript

The Voynich Manuscript is important because it is an enduring linguistic and historical puzzle: a 600-year-old illustrated codex written in a completely unknown, undeciphered script that has baffled generations of professional and amateur codebreakers, historians, and scientists

Unsolvable Code: Despite being studied by top military cryptanalysts from World War I and World War II (including William Friedman), no one has successfully translated the text or proved what language or cipher it uses

Linguistic Anomaly: Statistical analysis of the script reveals consistent internal linguistic patterns and rules, suggesting it represents a structured language or sophisticated artificial system rather than random gibberish—yet it matches no known human tongue.
